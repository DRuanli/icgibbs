\section{Problem setup}\label{sec:setup}
% Source: G4/PROOFS.md §0, G4/METHOD_SPEC.md §1, §3.
We observe features $X=(x_1,\dots,x_n)$ whose labels $y\in[C]^n$ are latent. As in the prior-fitted networks behind
TFMs \citep{muller2022pfn}, we assume an exchangeable prior: $\phi\sim p(\phi)$ and
$(x_i,y_i)\overset{\text{iid}}{\sim}p(x,y\mid\phi)$, with a test pair $(x_*,y_*)$ from the same mechanism. For a labelled
context $D$, the \emph{posterior predictive distribution} (PPD) of the prior is $p(y_*\mid x_*,D)$; a TFM returns an
approximation $\tfm(y_*\mid x_*,D)$ in one forward pass. Instead of $y$ we observe weak labels $\W$ with a known
likelihood $\lik(y)=p(\W\mid X,y)$, independent of $(x_*,y_*)$ given $(X,y)$ (A0). For \emph{partial labels} (PLL) each
point has a candidate set $S_i\ni y_i$ and $\lik(y)=\prod_i\mathbb 1[y_i\in S_i]$, under the standard assumption that the
probability of a candidate set does not depend on which member is true. For \emph{label proportions} (LLP) the points
form disjoint bags $B_g$ with observed label counts $w_g$, and
$\lik(y)=\prod_g\mathbb 1[\mathrm{counts}(y_{B_g})=w_g]$. PU data, label noise and semi-supervised learning are further
instance-level cases, $\lik(y)=\prod_i\lik_i(y_i)$ (\cref{app:secondary}). The targets are the posterior
over latent labels and the predictive,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
  \pi(y)=p(y\mid X,\W)\propto p(y\mid X)\,\lik(y),\qquad
  p(y_*\mid x_*,X,\W)=\textstyle\sum_y \pi'(y)\,p(y_*\mid x_*,(X,y)),
\end{equation}
with $\pi'(y)=p(y\mid x_*,X,\W)$. The analysis assumes (A1) an exact TFM, $\tfm=\ppd$, on every queried context;
(A2) a positive PPD; and (A3) $\sum_y p(y\mid X)\lik(y)>0$. \Cref{thm:stability} removes (A1).
