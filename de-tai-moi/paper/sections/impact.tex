\section*{Broader Impact Statement}
Learning from weak labels lowers the cost of annotation and makes coarse or ambiguous labels usable. Learning from label
proportions also recovers individual-level predictions from data released only in aggregate; when aggregation was
chosen to protect individuals, such models can weaken that protection, and releases of aggregated data should be
assessed with this in mind. We have not measured how much individual-level information the sampler recovers from bag
counts.
