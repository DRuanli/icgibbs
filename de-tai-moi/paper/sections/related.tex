\paragraph{Learning from weak labels.} Each type of weak supervision has its own family of learners, trained by
gradient descent with a loss tailored to the observation: for partial labels, progressive identification of the true
label \citep{proden}, consistent risk estimators \citep{feng2020provably} and contrastive disambiguation \citep{pico};
for label proportions, bag-level losses \citep{dllp,easy_llp}, consistency regularisation \citep{tsai2020llpvat} and
belief propagation over bags \citep{bp_llp}. Unified frameworks treat many types with one expectation--maximisation
scheme \citep{glws,ill,count_wsl}; GLWS computes the E-step exactly with an automaton over label sequences, under the
assumption that predictions are conditionally independent given the inputs. We share the goal of one procedure for many
weak-label types, but replace training by inference: the latent labels are sampled jointly, and their dependence is
modelled through the TFM's context rather than assumed away.

\paragraph{Tabular foundation models and in-context learning.} Prior-fitted networks are trained to approximate the
posterior predictive distribution of a prior over datasets \citep{muller2022pfn}; TabPFN \citep{hollmann2023tabpfn,tabpfn_nature}
and TabICL \citep{qu2025tabicl,tabicl_v2} turned this into strong off-the-shelf predictors for small tables, and recent
theory studies when transformers learn posterior predictives in context \citep{ppd_icl_theory}. Their behaviour with
imperfect context labels has been measured \citep{noise_immunity} and, for unlabelled context, analysed theoretically
\citep{unlabeled_icl_theory}; other work pretrains new in-context models for a single weak-label type, such as
positive--unlabelled data \citep{inctx_pu} or semi-supervised learning \citep{ic_ssl}. We instead keep a general TFM
frozen and use it as the conditional of Bayesian inference over latent labels, for any weak label with a known
observation model.

\paragraph{Gibbs sampling with learned conditionals.} Drawing labels from TabPFN's predictive has been used for
single-pass multiple imputation of a missing outcome \citep{sepin_mi}, and transformers have been pretrained to impute
missing features \citep{tabimpute}. Iterative imputation with chained conditional models
\citep{liu2014} and amortised Gibbs samplers \citep{apgs} are closest in spirit; our sampler conditions every label on all
others, uses the weak-label likelihood, and has guarantees even for incompatible conditionals.
